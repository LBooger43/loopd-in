Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

The short version
  • Loopd-In is a team app for salons. We keep your salon's information so your team can use it, and for nothing else.
  • We never sell information, never show ads, and never share it for marketing.
  • Each salon's information is walled off from every other salon.
  • Job applications go only to the managers of the salon you apply to.
  • You can ask us to see, fix or delete your information.

Who we are

Loopd-In ("we", "us") makes the Loopd-In app and the job apply pages that salons share. This policy covers the app, the apply pages, the demo and our website pages.

Two kinds of people use Loopd-In:

  • Salons and their teams (owners, managers, front desk and stylists) who sign in to the app.
  • Job applicants who fill out a salon's apply page.

Each salon decides what its team writes in the app and what to do with the applications it receives. We store and protect that information for the salon and act on the salon's instructions.

What we collect

From salons and their teams

  • Account details: name, email address, password (stored scrambled; we can't see it), role, and the salon's name, city and logo.
  • What your team adds: front desk notes, follow-ups, incidents, team chat and announcements, to-dos, supply lists, time-off requests, 1:1 notes, goal sheets, staff notes, write-ups and signatures, the employee handbook, flyers sent to print, and any photos attached to these.
  • Help requests: your message, any screenshot you attach, and basic details to help us fix problems: the page you were on, your device and browser type, screen size, app version and recent error messages.
  • Phone alerts: if you turn on notifications, your browser gives us a code that lets us send alerts to that device. It doesn't include your phone number.

From job applicants

  • Contact details: name, email, phone numbers, address and Instagram handle if given.
  • Application answers: positions wanted, availability, license, education, work history, references, and your typed signature.
  • Your résumé or a photo of it, if you add one.
  • Background questions (criminal record and controlled substances) appear only if the salon turns them on.
  • Salon managers may also add notes about you, or add your information if you walk in or call.

Automatically

  • Our hosting providers keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and time of request) for security and to keep the service running.
  • The app saves a few settings on your device, such as staying signed in. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show your team its notes, chat and lists, send applications to the right salon, and send the alerts you turned on.
  • To answer help requests and fix problems.
  • To keep the service secure and stop spam and abuse.
  • To manage your salon's account, such as beta dates and, later, billing.
  • To contact salon owners and managers about their account or important changes.

We don't sell information, use it for advertising, or use it to train artificial intelligence.

Who can see it

  • Your salon: people on a salon's team see that salon's information according to their role. For example, manager-only items like staff notes, write-ups and applications are hidden from stylists and front desk. People at other salons can never see it.
  • Loopd-In support: we look inside a salon's information only when the salon's owner or manager turns on support access in the app, and only for the time they choose. Every visit is listed for the salon to see.
  • Service providers that run parts of Loopd-In for us, under their own privacy and security commitments:
    • Supabase: database, file storage, sign-in and alerts (servers in the United States).
    • GitHub Pages: hosts the app's web pages.
    • Google Fonts and jsDelivr: deliver the app's fonts and code, which means your browser connects to them.
    • Apple, Google and Mozilla: deliver phone and browser alerts.
    • When card payments start, a payment company will handle billing. We'll never see or store full card numbers.
  • When the law requires it, or to protect people's safety or the security of the service.
  • If Loopd-In is sold or merged, information may move to the new owner, who must keep following this policy.

How long we keep it

  • We keep a salon's information while its account is open, including after a free beta ends, so nothing is lost if the salon continues.
  • Managers can delete notes, photos, applications and other items in the app at any time.
  • If a salon asks us to close its account, we delete its information within 90 days, except anything we must keep by law. Backups clear out on their normal schedule after that.

How we protect it

  • All connections are encrypted.
  • The database checks every request against the person's salon and role, so one salon's data can't be read by another.
  • Résumés and photos are stored privately and opened through short-lived links.
  • Passwords are stored scrambled.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we'll tell you as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

  • Team members: you can ask to see, correct or delete your information. Your salon's managers can also remove you from the team.
  • Job applicants: contact the salon you applied to, and its managers can update or delete your application. You can also contact us and we'll help.
  • Alerts: turn them off any time in the app or in your phone or browser settings.

Depending on where you live (for example California, Canada or the United Kingdom), you may have extra rights, such as knowing exactly what we hold about you. We honor these requests and won't treat you differently for making one.

Where information is stored

Loopd-In stores information in the United States. If you use Loopd-In from another country, your information is moved to and stored in the United States, where privacy laws may differ from those where you live.

Children

Loopd-In is a workplace tool and isn't meant for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is important, we'll tell salon owners in the app before it takes effect.

Contact us

  • Salons and teams: tap Help at the top of the Loopd-In app.
  • Job applicants: contact the salon you applied to, or ask them to reach us through the app.
